## 📱 Featured Project — NearChat

> A location-based social media app built with Flutter

**NearChat** connects people based on their real-world location — enabling hyper-local chat, a community feed, and nearby discovery. Built entirely with Flutter for seamless Android & iOS support.

### ✨ Highlights

- 📍 **Nearby Chat** — Message people around you in real time
- 🗺️ **Local Feed** — Posts and stories from your local area
- 🔍 **Discover** — Find nearby users and places
- 🎬 **Polished UI** — Lottie splash animations, dark/light theme, smooth transitions
- 🔐 **Auth Flow** — Secure login with persistent session management

### 🛠️ Built With

`Flutter` · `Dart` · `Lottie` · `SharedPreferences`
